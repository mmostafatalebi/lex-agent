# STATEMENT OF WORK

This Statement of Work (the "SOW") is entered into as of June 1, 2026 by and between Meridian Growth Partners ("Client") and the undersigned independent contractor ("Contractor"), and is governed by the Master Service Agreement between the Parties.

## 1. Scope of Services

Contractor shall provide design, development, and advisory services as needed, including but not limited to such tasks, deliverables, and responsibilities as the Client may identify from time to time over the course of the engagement. The scope of services is intended to be flexible and shall be understood to cover all work reasonably related to the project, whether or not specifically enumerated in this SOW.

## 2. Deliverables and Acceptance

Contractor shall deliver work product on an ongoing basis throughout the engagement. The Client shall review deliverables at its convenience. This SOW does not set out specific acceptance criteria, and whether any deliverable is acceptable shall be determined by the Client.

## 3. Change Control

The Client may, at any time and in its sole discretion, modify, expand, or redirect the scope of services, the deliverables, or the schedule by providing notice to Contractor, and Contractor shall accommodate such changes at no additional charge. Contractor may not modify the scope, the deliverables, or the schedule without the prior written consent of the Client.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Client shall pay Contractor a fixed monthly fee for the services. Payment for each month is conditioned upon the Client's satisfaction with the services provided during that month, as determined by the Client in its sole discretion. If the Client is not satisfied, the Client may reduce or withhold the fee for that month without any objective test of the quality of the work.

## 5. Term

This SOW commences on the date first written above and continues on a month-to-month basis until terminated by either Party on thirty (30) days' written notice. Either Party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured fifteen (15) days after written notice.

## 6. Independent Contractor

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of the Client. Contractor is responsible for its own taxes and shall not be entitled to any employee benefits. Nothing in this SOW creates a partnership or joint venture between the Parties.
